Lunio Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · applies to Lunio Pro on iPhone, iPad and Mac (formerly Lunio)
The short version: Lunio Pro collects nothing. Your notes live on your devices and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud database. Jent Innovations never sees them.

Who This Covers

This policy applies to Lunio Pro, a notebook app for iPhone, iPad and Mac published by Jent Innovations. Version 2.0 replaced the earlier Lunio app; it is no longer built with Thunkable. Other Jent Innovations apps have their own policies.

What We Collect

Nothing. Lunio Pro does not collect, analyze, sell or share personal data, usage data, telemetry or note content. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Storage & iCloud Sync

Your notebooks, pages, drawings and pictures are stored inside the app on each device. If iCloud sync is on (you can turn it off in Settings), they are copied to the private database of your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. Notebook, section and page titles and details are stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields. This data belongs to your Apple Account; Jent Innovations has no access to it.

On-Device Features

Recognising text inside pictures (so you can search it), document scanning and drawing all happen on your device. When you first open version 2.0, notes from the earlier Lunio app are brought over on your device too. Nothing is sent to us or to any other company.

Camera & Photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document into a page. Photos you add are picked by you and saved only into your notes.

Network Use

The only network connection Lunio Pro makes is to Apple's iCloud for sync, when it is on. Web links in your notes open in your browser only when you tap them.

Purchases

Lunio Pro is a paid app with no in-app purchases or advertising. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting a page moves it to the app's Trash for 30 days. Deleting the app removes its local data; your iCloud copy can be removed in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage ▸ Lunio Pro. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up, export or delete anything on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
