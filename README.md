# Jent Innovations — legal & policy pages

Public home of the privacy policies and terms for apps published by **Jent Innovations**, an independent app studio.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`index.html`](index.html) | General privacy policy (default for apps without their own) |
| [`forge.html`](forge.html) | Privacy policy for **Forge** (iPhone 3D-print slicer) |
| [`terms.html`](terms.html) | Terms & conditions for **MotorScribe** |
| [`apps.html`](apps.html) | Directory of apps and which policy covers each |

These files are served as static pages. **Do not rename or move them**: the URLs are referenced from App Store / Google Play listings and in-app links.

## Working in this repo

- [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md): dated history of every policy change. Add an entry whenever policy text changes.
- [`docs/ADDING-AN-APP.md`](docs/ADDING-AN-APP.md): checklist for publishing a policy for a new app.
- [`docs/LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md`](docs/LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md): what a small app studio needs beyond a privacy policy. Not legal advice.
- A GitHub Action checks every link on each push and weekly.
