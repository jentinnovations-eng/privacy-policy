Life2STL — Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026
The short version: Life2STL processes your photos and scans on your device. Jent Innovations collects nothing. If you use iCloud, your saved models sync through your own private iCloud account.

Who This Covers

This policy applies to Life2STL, an app that turns objects you scan, and photos, into printable 3D models, published by Jent Innovations. Other Jent Innovations apps have their own policies.

What We Collect

Jent Innovations does not collect, sell or share your personal information through Life2STL. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What Life2STL Stores

  • STL files and preview pictures of models you save to the Library
  • Your printer, filament and export choices

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also kept in your own private iCloud account so it appears on your other iPhones. Jent Innovations cannot see or access your iCloud data; Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud. Turn off iCloud for Life2STL in iOS Settings to keep everything on one iPhone.

The photos taken for a Quick 3D model or a Detailed Scan are used to build the model on your iPhone and are discarded when it is done. They are not saved to your photo library or uploaded.

Permissions

Life2STL may ask for the following. Each is optional and you can change it in iOS Settings at any time.

  • Camera: used to take the photo you want to convert and the photos for a Quick 3D model or a Detailed Scan.
  • Photos: you choose individual images with the system photo picker.

Network Use

Life2STL processes photos and scans on your device using Apple's Vision, ARKit and Object Capture frameworks. Photos are never uploaded. Life2STL has no servers and no networking code of its own; the only data it sends anywhere is the iCloud sync described above, which goes to your own iCloud account through Apple.

Purchases

Life2STL has no purchases or advertising.

Sharing and Export

You can export STL, OBJ files with the iOS share sheet. Where they go is your choice.

Your Choices

You can delete any model in the Library (this also removes it from iCloud and your other iPhones), revoke permissions in iOS Settings, or delete the app to remove its local data. To remove Life2STL's iCloud data, go to Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up, export or delete anything on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
