StickerCraft — Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026
The short version: StickerCraft collects nothing. Your images and designs stay on your Mac.

Who This Covers

This policy applies to StickerCraft, a sticker and cut-file design app for Mac, published by Jent Innovations. Other Jent Innovations apps have their own policies.

What We Collect

Jent Innovations does not collect, sell or share your personal information through StickerCraft. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What Stays on Your Mac

  • Images you import into your design library
  • Your sticker designs and settings

This data is kept in StickerCraft's own folder on your Mac (in ~/Library/Containers/com.Jentinnovations.StickerCraft). Moving the app to the Trash does not delete that folder; delete it yourself to remove your library. It may be included in your own Time Machine or iCloud backups.

Permissions

StickerCraft asks for no system permissions. It uses only:

  • Files you choose: StickerCraft opens only the images you pick and saves only where you choose in the save panel.

Network Use

StickerCraft contains no networking code. Background removal and tracing run on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework. "Open in Cricut Design Space" hands your exported SVG to that app on your Mac; anything it does with the file is covered by Cricut's own policy.

Purchases

StickerCraft has no purchases or advertising.

Sharing and Export

You can export SVG cut files and 300 DPI PNG images to any folder you choose.

Your Choices

You can delete any design inside the app, or delete the folder above to remove all of its local data. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up, export or delete anything on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions regarding privacy: jentinnovations@gmail.com.
