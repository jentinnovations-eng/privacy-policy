AutoMod 3D — Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026
The short version: AutoMod 3D collects nothing. Your photos, scans and builds stay on your device.

Who This Covers

This policy applies to AutoMod 3D, a 3D vehicle customizer and scanning app, published by Jent Innovations. Other Jent Innovations apps have their own policies.

What We Collect

Jent Innovations does not collect, sell or share your personal information through AutoMod 3D. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What Stays on Your Device

  • Vehicle builds and custom parts you create
  • Photos you take or import for scans and custom parts
  • 3D models built from your photos

This data is kept in the app's private storage. Deleting the app removes it. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups under your device settings.

Permissions

AutoMod 3D may ask for the following. Each is optional and you can change it in iOS Settings at any time.

  • Camera: used only while you take walkaround photos for a scan.
  • Photos: used to import a photo you choose, and to save studio snapshots when you ask.
  • Motion: used for the level guide while taking walkaround photos.

Network Use

AutoMod 3D has no servers of its own. 3D scans are built on your device by Apple's Object Capture. The only network use is the App Store, when you view or buy AutoMod Pro.

Purchases

AutoMod Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as subscriptions and a one-time purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether Pro is active.

Sharing and Export

You can export models (USDZ, OBJ, STL) and snapshots with the iOS share sheet or save them to Photos.

Your Choices

You can delete any data inside the app, revoke permissions in iOS Settings, or delete the app to remove all of its local data. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up, export or delete anything on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions regarding privacy: jentinnovations@gmail.com.
