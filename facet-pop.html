Facet Pop — Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026
The short version: Facet Pop has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of ours. Your progress stays on your device.

Who This Covers

This policy applies to Facet Pop, a match-3 puzzle game published by Jent Innovations. Other Jent Innovations apps have their own policies.

What We Collect

Jent Innovations does not collect, sell or share your personal information through Facet Pop. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What Stays on Your Device

  • Your level progress, stars, coins, hearts, daily streak and achievements
  • Your settings (dark mode, sound, haptics) and unlocked tile themes

This data is kept in the app's private storage. Deleting the app removes it. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups under your device settings.

Permissions

Facet Pop does not request any permissions.

Network Use

Facet Pop only goes online to talk to the App Store for purchases. We run no servers and receive no data.

Purchases

Facet Pop offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs and a one-time Facet Pro upgrade (unlimited hearts). Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can restore a Facet Pro purchase from the Shop screen. The game is fully playable without purchases, and coins can also be earned by playing.

Sharing and Export

Facet Pop has no sharing features.

Children

Facet Pop does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your Choices

You can delete the app to remove all of its local data. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up, export or delete anything on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
