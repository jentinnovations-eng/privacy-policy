JENT INNOVATIONS Privacy Policy

Legal

Your privacy
matters to us.

We build tools that help you move, plan, and grow. We don't sell your data, show you ads, or track you across the web. This policy covers every app we make.

Last updated: May 30, 2026

01

Who We Are

Jent Innovations is an independent app studio building productivity and fitness tools. All apps published under the Jent Innovations name are covered by this policy.

Contact us any time at jentinnovations@gmail.com

02

What Data We Collect

We only collect data you enter directly into our apps. Depending on the app, this may include:

  • Fitness and workout data (exercises, sets, reps, weights, logs)
  • App preferences and settings (such as dark mode or notification preferences)
  • Schedules, plans, or other content you create inside the app
We do not collect your name, email address, location, payment details, or any other personal identifying information unless you contact us directly.

03

How We Use Your Data

All data you enter is used solely to provide the app's functionality — nothing else. We do not:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data to build profiles or target you with ads
  • Transmit your data to any server we control for analytics

04

Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and, where applicable, through our app platform provider Thunkable. Thunkable provides the backend infrastructure for our apps. You can review Thunkable's own privacy practices at thunkable.com/privacy.

We do not operate our own servers or databases outside of Thunkable's platform.

05

Third-Party Services

Our apps may rely on the following third-party services to function:

  • Thunkable — app platform and database provider
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — app distribution platforms (subject to their own privacy policies)
  • Expo / React Native — open-source mobile development framework used to build our apps

We do not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or social media SDKs in any of our apps.

06

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to one of our apps, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our apps after any changes means you accept the updated policy.

This single policy covers all current and future Jent Innovations apps — you'll never need to find a separate policy for each app we release.
