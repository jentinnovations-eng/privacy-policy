Forge Slicer — Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026
The short version: Forge collects nothing. It has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics tracking, and no networking code. Your 3D models and G-code never leave your iPhone.

Who This Covers

This policy applies to Forge, an on-device 3D printing slicer for iPhone published by Jent Innovations. Other Jent Innovations apps are covered by their respective policies.

What We Collect

Nothing. Forge does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal information. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising framework, and no remote account system. The application contains no networking code.

What Stays on Your Device

Forge executes 100% locally on your iPhone hardware. The following data is kept solely in private sandboxed storage and never transmitted:

  • 3D model files you import (.stl) and your local model library
  • Model position, orientation, scaling, and rotation data
  • Printer profiles, slicing presets, layer heights, and material parameters
  • Light/dark appearance preferences
  • Generated machine G-code files

Deleting Forge cleanly removes all stored files. No cloud duplicates exist.

Files and STL Processing

When you open an STL file in Forge, it is processed in local memory. Exported G-code is handed directly to the iOS system share sheet (AirDrop, Files, or USB storage) under your explicit control.

Third-Party Components

Forge incorporates the open-source Clipper2 geometry library (Boost Software License 1.0), which executes locally without network access.
