Forge — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026

The short version: Forge collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no network code. Your models never leave your iPhone.

Who this covers

This policy applies to Forge, a 3D printing slicer for iPhone published by Jent Innovations. It applies to Forge only. Other Jent Innovations apps are covered by their own policies.

What we collect

Nothing. Forge does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal information. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising framework, and no account system. The app contains no networking code.

What stays on your device

Forge works entirely on your iPhone. The following are stored locally, in the app's own private storage, and are never transmitted anywhere:

  • 3D model files you import, and the model library you build from them
  • Position, scale, and rotation for each model
  • Your printer profile and slicing settings
  • Your appearance preference (light or dark)
  • G-code files that Forge generates

Deleting Forge removes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, and we have no way to recover it — which is the trade-off that comes with an app that keeps nothing.

Files you open

When you open an STL file in Forge, the file is read and processed on your device. When you export G-code or a model, iOS asks you where to put it. Forge never uploads a file, and it does not read files you have not explicitly opened or imported.

Tracking

Forge does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the Advertising Identifier, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. There is nothing to opt out of.

Third parties

Forge includes one open-source geometry library (Clipper2, Boost Software License 1.0) which runs entirely on your device and performs no networking. No other third-party code is included. If you purchase anything in the app, that transaction is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Jent Innovations receives sales reports from Apple that do not identify you.

Children

Forge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal data held about you. Jent Innovations holds no personal data about Forge users, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. Your data is on your device and under your control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Forge adds a feature that changes any of the above — cloud sync, for example, or sending G-code to a networked printer — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else about Forge: jentinnovations@gmail.com.
